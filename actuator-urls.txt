========================================
  Spring Boot Actuator 监控地址
========================================

【基础地址】
http://localhost:8080/actuator

【核心监控】
健康检查: http://localhost:8080/actuator/health
应用信息: http://localhost:8080/actuator/info
监控指标: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics
环境变量: http://localhost:8080/actuator/env
Bean列表: http://localhost:8080/actuator/beans
接口映射: http://localhost:8080/actuator/mappings
日志级别: http://localhost:8080/actuator/loggers
线程转储: http://localhost:8080/actuator/threaddump

【数据库连接池 - HikariCP】
活跃连接数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/hikaricp.connections.active
空闲连接数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/hikaricp.connections.idle
最大连接数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/hikaricp.connections.max
最小连接数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/hikaricp.connections.min
连接超时次数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/hikaricp.connections.timeout.count
等待连接线程数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/hikaricp.connections.pending

【HTTP 请求统计】
请求总数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/http.server.requests
各接口请求统计: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/http.server.requests?tag=uri:/api/user/list

【JVM 内存】
堆内存使用: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/jvm.memory.used
堆内存最大值: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/jvm.memory.max
非堆内存使用: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/jvm.memory.non.heap.used
GC暂停时间: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/jvm.gc.pause

【CPU 使用率】
系统CPU使用率: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/system.cpu.usage
进程CPU使用率: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/process.cpu.usage
系统负载(1分钟): http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/system.load.average.1m

【Redis】
Redis连接池活跃连接: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/redis.connections.active
Redis命令执行次数: http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/redis.commands

========================================
  快速测试命令
========================================

# 健康检查
curl http://localhost:8080/actuator/health

# 查看连接池状态
curl http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/hikaricp.connections.active

# 查看JVM内存
curl http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/jvm.memory.used
